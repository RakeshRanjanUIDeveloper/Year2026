var — hoisted and initialized with undefined:

  • hoisted to the top of its scope
  • initialized with undefined automatically
  • can be accessed before declaration, returns undefined

let — hoisted but NOT initialized (TDZ):

  • hoisted to the top of its scope
  • NOT initialized — sits in TDZ (Temporal Dead Zone)
  • cannot be accessed before declaration, throws ReferenceError

const — same as let:

  • hoisted to the top of its scope
  • NOT initialized — sits in TDZ
  • cannot be accessed before declaration, throws ReferenceError
  • must be initialized at declaration

TDZ (Temporal Dead Zone):

  • the period between hoisting and the actual declaration line
  • only applies to let and const
  • variable exists but cannot be touched during this period
